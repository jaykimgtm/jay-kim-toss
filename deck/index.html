09 · First 90 days

What I’d do in my first 90 days

  1. Days 1 ~ 30

    Learn and take the load

    • Learn the bar and interview loops
    • Meet every hiring manager I support
    • Run live reqs in Toss’s own loops and tools

    Outcome

    Pipelines moving, HMs aligned

  2. Days 31 ~ 60

    Build the engine

    • Outbound playbook per role family
    • Referral loop and interviewer pre-briefs
    • Small, well-briefed agency bench

    Outcome

    A repeatable sourcing engine

  3. Days 61 ~ 90

    Deliver and scale

    • Hires closed, offers out
    • Funnel metrics on a set cadence
    • Talent reach in Korea, Japan and new markets

    Outcome

    Hires closing, metrics visible
